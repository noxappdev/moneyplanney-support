Common questions

I forgot my passcode

MoneyPlanney has no account and no cloud, so nobody can reset it for you. Your data is safe but can only be opened with your passcode. Try again as often as you like: nothing is erased after wrong attempts. If you cannot remember it, you can erase the data on the phone and restore a backup file. You will need that backup's own password, which is separate.

I lost my phone

If you exported a backup file before, install MoneyPlanney on the new phone and choose Restore from a backup on the first screen. Without a backup, the data cannot be recovered. This is why we suggest a backup once a month (Settings → Back up and export).

I forgot a backup password

A backup file cannot be opened without its password and we cannot recover it. Keep the password somewhere safe, apart from the file.

Is my data sent to the cloud?

No. Everything stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I open my entries in a spreadsheet?

Settings → Back up and export → Export for a spreadsheet creates a CSV file that Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets can open.

Why did my safe-to-spend number change?

It is what is left in the envelopes that count toward it, divided by the days until payday, rounded down. Adding an expense lowers it. A new payday resets it. You choose which envelopes count in Settings → Today and entries.
